## Executive Summary

UrEducator 2.0 has achieved an operational milestone: the **Computer-Based Testing (CBT) engine, administrative lifecycle, role-based access control, server-side scoring, and security rules are fully implemented, verified, and accepted on live staging infrastructure (`ugc-mock-test-staging`)**.

However, the project is **NOT READY for student launch** due to a fundamental gap:
1. **Academic Content is 100% Unverified / Synthetic:** The live staging platform is populated exclusively with synthetic test fixtures (4 subjects, 150 dummy questions). The official UGC NET curriculum skeleton in `docs/M9_OFFICIAL_CURRICULUM_DATA.json` is at level V1 (unverified by human eyes), and contains **zero authentic questions, zero official syllabus statements, and 12 declared research gaps**.
2. **Infrastructure Readiness is Bounded to Staging:** Critical pre-production operational controls—including Razorpay live payment gateway activation, App Check enforcement, automated Firestore backups, and production project provisioning (`ugc-mock-test`)—remain deliberately disabled or unconfigured.

The shortest, safest route to launch is a **staged 3-phase progression**:
- **Phase A (Private Pilot):** Complete M9.0 human source capture and dual-verification of Paper 1 + author 50 authentic pilot questions with verified explanations (M9.4) -> Launch controlled invite-only pilot with payments disabled.
- **Phase B (Public Free Beta):** Enforce App Check + activate public legal notices and support workflows -> Open free beta for Paper 1.
- **Phase C (Paid Commercial Launch):** Configure live Razorpay credentials, automated backups, and production isolation -> Deploy to `ugc-mock-test`.
